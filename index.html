CLEETS-KG v1.3.0 · 96,479 triples

CLEETS knowledge graph, drawn two ways

CLEETS-KG integrates eight public datasets on electric-vehicle keepership, public charging devices, population, geography and deprivation for the local authority districts of Wales (with population density for England too) under one ontology. These two pages show the graph from different distances; both are generated from the same Turtle file and cross-checked against it.
